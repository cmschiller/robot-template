Phase 5 of 6: referee report.

You did not write this paper. You are a referee for the Journal of Finance. Read paper/paper.tex
(and paper/paper.pdf), the scripts in code/, DECISIONS.md, and memo/memo.md, the memo the paper was
built from.

1. Check at least three numbers in the paper against the code and its output, and at least three
   references against the web. Say what you checked and what you found.
2. Write review/ai-referee.md:
   - a summary of the paper in three sentences;
   - your recommendation: reject, major revision, minor revision, or accept;
   - the main concerns, ranked by importance: for each, where it is, why it matters, and what would
     fix it;
   - minor comments.
3. Don't change anything outside review/. Commit.

When the report is written, create the file .phase-done with one line giving your recommendation.
